## ☁️ How to Create Your AWS Account

If you have not created an AWS account yet, do this **before arriving**:

1. Go to **https://aws.amazon.com**
2. Click **Create an AWS Account**
3. Enter your email address and choose an account name
4. Enter your payment details *(a $1 temporary hold may appear — it gets reversed)*
5. Complete phone or email verification
6. Select the **Free Tier** plan
7. Sign in to confirm it works: **https://console.aws.amazon.com**

> ⚠️ Do not create your account on the day of the workshop. It may delay your participation.
