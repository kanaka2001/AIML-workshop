## ☁️ How to Create Your AWS Account

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/93/Amazon_Web_Services_Logo.svg" width="100" alt="AWS"/>
</p>

If you have not created an AWS account yet, follow these steps **before the workshop day**:

1. Go to **[https://aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com)**
2. Click **Create an AWS Account**
3. Enter your email address and choose an account name
4. Enter payment details — a temporary $1 hold may appear and will be reversed
5. Complete phone or email verification
6. Select the **Free Tier** plan
7. Confirm your account works at **[https://console.aws.amazon.com](https://console.aws.amazon.com)**

> ⚠️ **Do not create your account on the day of the workshop.** On-the-spot setup can delay your participation in hands-on labs.
