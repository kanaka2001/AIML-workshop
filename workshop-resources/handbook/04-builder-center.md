## 🔗 What is AWS Builder Center?

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/93/Amazon_Web_Services_Logo.svg" width="80" alt="AWS"/>
  <br/>
  <b>builder.aws.com</b>
</p>

AWS Builder Center is the official online home for the AWS community. It gives you:

| Section | What You Get |
|---|---|
| **Learn** | Structured learning paths, training courses, and tutorials |
| **Build** | Hands-on labs and project guides |
| **Community** | Connect with builders globally, publish articles, join discussions |
| **Toolbox** | SDKs, developer tools, and resources |

## 🎁 Student Rewards — Up to $579 in Value

When you sign up and verify your student status through AWS Builder Center, you unlock real rewards:

### Step 1 — Sign Up & Verify
Sign up using the link below and complete your student verification through **SheerID** (name, college, email). Most verifications complete in minutes.

**Reward:** 1 year of Premium AWS Skill Builder access — **$449 value, free.**

### Step 2 — Complete Your Profile
Add a photo and an about section to your Builder Center profile.

### Step 3 — Earn Badges & Unlock More

As you engage with the community, you earn badges that unlock additional rewards:

| Badges Earned | Reward |
|---|---|
| **7 badges** | $10 in AWS credits |
| **14 badges** | Additional $20 in AWS credits |
| **21 badges** | AWS Foundational Certification exam voucher — **$100 value** |

**How to earn badges:**
- Publish articles on AWS Builder Center
- Comment on community content
- Maintain activity streaks

> No credit card needed to join. No AWS account required for Builder Center.
