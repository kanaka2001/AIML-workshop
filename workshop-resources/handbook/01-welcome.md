## 🎯 What This Session Is About

This session introduces you to the people and community behind this workshop — the **AWS Student Builder Group (SBG) – DBIT** — and sets the stage for everything you will build over the next two days.

---

## 👥 Who is AWS Student Builder Group – DBIT?

AWS Student Builder Group – DBIT is a **student-driven community** at Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. It runs in collaboration with the **Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning**.

The community's mission is simple:

> *Help students move beyond theoretical knowledge and start building real solutions on the cloud.*

What the community does:
- Organizes workshops, hackathons, and technical events
- Connects students with AWS learning resources and career pathways
- Creates peer learning opportunities through collaboration
- Helps students build skills relevant to internships and careers

---

## 🎪 About This Event

**AWS Builders Lab** is part of **HexaVerse CloudFest '26** — the department's annual technology celebration.

This workshop is:
- **Two days** of hands-on, instructor-led learning
- **Beginner-friendly** — no prior cloud experience needed
- **Completely practical** — you will deploy real resources on AWS
